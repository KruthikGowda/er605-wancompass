        How WANCompass measures

        Every 10 seconds the Raspberry Pi pings Cloudflare, Google and Quad9 through each connection separately (the ER605 pins each test address to one ISP). "Speed of response" is the round-trip time in milliseconds: lower is better. Packet loss is the share of pings that got no answer. WANCompass learns what's normal for each ISP, so a connection is called slow when it's much slower than its own usual, not just slower than the other.
